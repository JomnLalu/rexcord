What you'll build

Six modules. Every one ships something that actually runs by the time you close your laptop.

01

It's Alive

Your bot logs into Discord and answers its first command.

!ping
02

Personality

Dice rolls, a magic 8-ball, random picks — your bot gets an attitude.

!roll · !8ball
03

Listening

It reads the channel and reacts to what people say, on its own.

on_message
04

Web Data

Pull live data from the internet and post it as a rich embed.

!pokemon
05

Polls & Moderation

Run polls, show profiles, and clean up chat — safely.

!poll · !clear
06

Minigame

Rock, paper, scissors — with real clickable buttons.

!rps
